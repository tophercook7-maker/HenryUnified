Connect your OpenAI account for Henry's most powerful features:

Get your OpenAI API Key:

  1. Visit platform.openai.com/api-keys
  2. Sign in to your OpenAI account
  3. Click "+ Create new secret key"
  4. Name your key "Henry AI"
  5. Copy the generated key (you'll only see it once!)

⚠️ This key enables Henry to access powerful AI models. Store it securely.
