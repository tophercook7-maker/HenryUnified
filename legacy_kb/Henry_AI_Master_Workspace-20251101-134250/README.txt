Henry AI — Master Workspace
============================
How to use this kit:
1) Upload this folder to your Google Drive.
2) Store active documents only here; archive older files elsewhere.
3) Use the naming pattern below for every file to keep things tidy.

Naming Pattern:
Henry AI – <Document Purpose> – <Month Year> v<version>
Example: Henry AI – Investor Deck – Oct 2025 v3
